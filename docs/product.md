# Albus — Product Overview

## Product thesis

Marketplace commerce becomes operationally expensive when every sales channel has its own cabinet, product-card requirements, stock logic, order flow and error states.

**Albus** reduces that fragmentation by giving sellers one AI-assisted operating workspace across multiple marketplaces.

## Who it is for

- small and medium marketplace sellers;
- multi-channel e-commerce businesses;
- marketplace managers;
- distributors and brands;
- e-commerce agencies managing several seller accounts.

## Core product modules

### AI Product Content
Helps sellers prepare and improve marketplace-ready product information while reducing repetitive manual completion.

Publicly described capabilities include:

- product-card creation assistance;
- listing enrichment and modernization;
- category and attribute assistance;
- content consistency across sales channels.

The production enrichment rules, prompts, marketplace mappings and validation strategies are proprietary and are not included in this repository.

### Multi-Marketplace Workspace
Provides a unified operational view for connected marketplace accounts, including products, orders, stock, prices and account health.

### Seller Diagnostics
Surfaces operational signals such as critical issues, marketplace connection health and products requiring attention so sellers can prioritize work faster.

### AI Assistant
Allows sellers to work with marketplace information using natural-language requests. The assistant is designed to use connected seller data rather than invent marketplace state.

Illustrative user requests:

- “Show today’s critical issues.”
- “Which products require attention?”
- “Show products with low stock.”
- “Prepare this product for the connected marketplaces.”

### Controlled Operations
For eligible actions, Albus is designed to separate AI assistance from unrestricted execution. Sensitive operations remain behind product controls and seller authorization.

## Target marketplaces

Initial focus:

- Yandex Market;
- Uzum Market;
- Ozon;
- Wildberries.

Future expansion may include Amazon, eBay and Shopify.

## Customer value

Albus is designed to create value through:

- less repetitive marketplace work;
- faster product preparation;
- fewer operational mistakes;
- better visibility across seller accounts;
- lower cost of managing multiple channels;
- a consistent workflow as the seller adds marketplaces.

## Business model

**Subscription SaaS.** Commercial plans can vary by connected marketplace accounts, product volume, AI usage, automation level and B2B requirements.

## Current stage

**MVP.** The product direction and core workflows are established, with current development focused on integration reliability, unified marketplace behavior, pilot readiness and commercial rollout.

## Market direction

The first target market is **Uzbekistan**, followed by expansion toward **Central Asia and CIS** markets.

## Product vision

**One seller. Multiple marketplaces. One operating system.**

The goal is to move sellers away from fragmented marketplace administration toward a single AI-native operating layer for commerce.
