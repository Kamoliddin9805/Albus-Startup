# Product Overview

## What Albus solves

Marketplace sellers frequently repeat the same work across several platforms: creating listings, filling attributes, checking stock, monitoring orders, fixing errors and switching between seller cabinets.

Albus reduces this fragmentation by providing a unified workspace supported by AI.

## Main product modules

### AI Product Content
- Analyze a product from its name and available data
- Prepare marketplace-ready titles and attributes
- Assist with category and required-field completion
- Modernize incomplete product cards

### Multi-Marketplace Operations
- Unified view of connected seller accounts
- Products, stock, price and order workflows
- Marketplace-specific capability handling through adapters

### Diagnostics
- Today’s operational status
- Critical issues
- Products requiring attention
- Marketplace connection and API health

### AI Assistant
The assistant is designed to answer seller questions using connected marketplace data and to prepare safe actions when the user asks Albus to make a change.

Example commands:

- “Show today’s critical issues.”
- “Which products require attention?”
- “Prepare this product for all connected marketplaces.”
- “Show products with low stock.”

## Target users

- Small and medium marketplace sellers
- Multi-channel e-commerce businesses
- Marketplace managers
- Distributors
- E-commerce agencies

## Initial market

Uzbekistan, with further expansion toward Central Asia and CIS markets.

## Business model

Subscription SaaS. Pricing can vary by connected marketplace accounts, product volume, AI usage and automation level.

## Current stage

MVP / pilot readiness. Core workflows have been defined and the product is being hardened for reliable multi-marketplace operation.
